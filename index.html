About Winsteel

Engineering Built on Strength, Precision & Trust

Winsteel Engineering Works Pvt. Ltd. is an industry-leading manufacturer of Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings (PEB) and heavy structural steel solutions. We specialize in the turnkey design, 3D TEKLA detailing, automated CNC fabrication, and rapid on-site erection of industrial warehouses, manufacturing facilities, clear-span sheds, and specialized heavy infrastructure systems.

Operating from our advanced 200,000+ sq. ft. heavy fabrication facility in Taloja, Navi Mumbai, we partner with premier industrial developers, logistics majors, and EPC leaders including L&T, Tata Projects, and Afcons — delivering projects 40% faster with unmatched structural safety.
