Engineering Excellence Built on Precision

Over 35 years of benchmark manufacturing for India's monumental transit and bridge infrastructure.

Pioneering Heavy Engineering

Mastering the Dynamics of Heavy Steel & Falsework Systems

Winsteel Engineering Works Pvt. Ltd. was founded on an uncompromised commitment to engineering integrity. Today, as India embarks on generational infrastructure programs—including high-speed bullet train corridors, trans-harbour sea links, and multi-tier metro viaducts—Winsteel provides the heavy steel sinews that make rapid construction possible.

Our state-of-the-art facility in Sachin GIDC, Surat houses specialized plate cutting, submerged arc welding booms, multi-axis horizontal floor borers, and proof-load testing rigs. We ensure that every piece of machinery delivered to a contractor's casting yard or launching front performs with millimeter precision.

Our Capabilities

End-to-End Heavy Engineering

CAD / CAM & FEA Design

In-house parametric modeling, dynamic wind and hydrostatic stress analysis, and kinematic simulation of self-launching cycles.

Heavy High-Tensile Fabrication

Handling plates up to 100mm thickness in high-tensile E350/E450 steel with automatic SAW and flux-cored arc welding.

Hydraulics & PLC Integration

Turnkey electro-hydraulic power units, synchronized multi-cylinder jacking systems, and cabin control panels.

Jobsite Erection & Training

Field engineers supervise full site assembly, commissioning trials, proof load load-cells, and operator safety certifications.
