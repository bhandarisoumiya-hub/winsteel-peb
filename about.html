Pioneering Heavy Engineering

Engineering Expertise Behind Every PEB Structure

WINSTEEL Engineering Works Pvt. Ltd. brings more than five decades of engineering and manufacturing experience to the Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB) sector. Established in 1972, the company has built its capabilities through engineering and manufacturing custom-made equipment, formwork, falsework, and heavy steel solutions for construction and infrastructure applications.Today, WINSTEEL provides an in-house PEB solution covering structural engineering, detailing, steel fabrication and project-specific building requirements. Our approach combines established manufacturing experience with the technical requirements of modern steel building construction.

Our manufacturing facility in Taloja, Navi Mumbai supports the fabrication and assembly of PEB structural components. From primary and secondary structural members to project-specific steelwork, each stage is carried out against defined engineering, fabrication and quality requirements.

Our Capabilities

End-to-End Heavy Engineering

Structural Engineering & Design

PEB structural design based on building geometry, loading conditions, clear-span requirements, site conditions and applicable design standards.

Steel Fabrication

Controlled fabrication of PEB structural components, including cutting, drilling, welding, assembly and finishing as required by the project.

Quality Control & Inspection

Quality checks throughout material handling, fabrication, welding and assembly to support dimensional accuracy, material compliance and fabrication quality.

PEB Building Components

Manufacturing and integration of primary and secondary structural members, roofing and wall systems, connections and other project-specific PEB components.

Project Execution

Coordination of engineering, detailing, fabrication, supply and erection activities according to the agreed project scope.
