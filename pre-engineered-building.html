STRUCTURAL DEFINITION

What is a Pre-Engineered Building?

A Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) is an engineered steel building system in which the structural components are designed and detailed according to project requirements and manufactured under controlled factory conditions before being transported to the project site for erection.

An Integrated Structural System

A typical PEB consists of primary structural framing, secondary framing, roof and wall systems, connections and associated building components. The structural system is engineered to accommodate applicable loads and building requirements while allowing the configuration to be adapted to the intended use of the facility.

Controlled Factory vs. Site Fabrication

Unlike conventional construction methods where a greater proportion of structural fabrication may take place at site, PEB construction transfers significant fabrication and preparation activities to a controlled manufacturing environment. The completed components are then transported to site for assembly and erection.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Pre-Engineered Buildings FAQ

Answers to the most frequent technical and commercial inquiries regarding PEB projects in India.

PEBs are erected up to 40% to 50% faster because structural components are pre-fabricated in an automated, factory-controlled environment while on-site civil and foundation work happens simultaneously. When the steel members arrive on site, they are assembled strictly with high-strength bolts, eliminating slow on-site cutting, fitting, formwork curing, and scaffolding cycles.
Winsteel can design and fabricate clear-span portal frames up to 90 meters (approx. 295 feet) without any intermediate columns. For facilities wider than 90 meters, multi-span rigid frames with widely-spaced interior columns are utilized to achieve total building widths exceeding 200 to 300 meters.
Yes. We regularly design PEB facilities supporting overhead Electric Overhead Traveling (EOT) cranes ranging from 5 Metric Tonnes up to 100 Metric Tonnes. Our structural calculations integrate dynamic wheel impact forces, lateral surge brackets, stepped built-up columns, and crane runway girder designs compliant with CMAA and IS 800 standards.
We provide high-density Polyurethane (PUF), Polyisocyanurate (PIR), and Rockwool insulated sandwich panels ranging from 50mm to 100mm in thickness. These panels have exceptional thermal R-values that block radiant external heat, lowering indoor ambient temperatures by 6°C to 10°C in peak Indian summers and reducing factory HVAC energy costs by up to 35%.
Yes, modular scalability is one of the biggest advantages of PEB. Endwall framing is engineered to easily allow future length extensions simply by unbolting the endwall panels and adding additional portal frame bays without disrupting ongoing factory operations.
All raw steel plates and coils come with original Mill Test Certificates (MTC). Primary framing is shot-blasted to SA 2.5 profile and protected with high-build epoxy zinc phosphate primers and polyurethane finishes. For roofing and cladding, our Galvalume AZ150 pre-painted sheets offer a 25-year anti-perforation warranty against corrosion.
