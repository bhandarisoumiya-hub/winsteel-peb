Three Decades of Engineering Groundwork

From precision tooling to mammoth launching gantries spanning India's widest rivers and coastal straits.

1991

Foundation & Precision Machining Workshop

Winsteel Engineering Works was established by visionary structural engineers with a focus on custom machined steel components and high-accuracy press tooling for heavy industrial clients in Maharashtra.

2002

Entry into Highway & Elevated Metro Formworks

Recognizing the onset of India's Golden Quadrilateral expressway program, Winsteel expanded into heavy structural formworks, developing match-casting segment moulds and pier shutters for early flyover networks.

2012

First 1,000 MT Segmental Launching Gantry Commissioned

Winsteel achieved a national milestone with the complete in-house fabrication and hydraulic commissioning of its first 1,000-tonne automated overhead launching gantry for an interstate highway viaduct.

2018

Inauguration of Taloja MIDC Mega Fabrication Facility

Consolidating operations into an expansive 200,000+ sq. ft. industrial campus in Taloja, equipped with 120-tonne tandem EOT cranes, underwater CNC plasma tables, and automated shot-blasting bays.

2023

Marine Viaduct Gantries on Atal Setu (MTHL)

Fabricated and site-commissioned dual 1,200 MT marine launching gantries for India's longest open-sea bridge over Mumbai Bay, achieving record span erection intervals under extreme saltwater wind loads.

2026 & Beyond

High-Speed Rail & Automated Falsework Innovation

Expanding into 1,800 MT smart launching gantries with digital load telemetry, high-speed rail monolithic moulds, and expanding exports to the Middle East and Southeast Asian infrastructure sectors.

Forming The Future with high-integrity steel engineering.
