Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Everything you need to know about Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings (PEB), construction costs, engineering methodology, materials, and turnkey execution.

A Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) is a modern structural steel building that is custom-designed, engineered, and manufactured in a high-precision factory (off-site) and rapidly assembled at the project location using bolted connections.

Unlike conventional buildings where fabricated steel or RCC concrete casting occurs slowly on-site, PEBs comprise pre-cut, pre-punched, and pre-welded primary built-up frames, cold-formed secondary C/Z purlins, and insulated standing-seam roof/wall cladding. They are the global industry standard for warehouses, manufacturing plants, clear-span industrial sheds, multi-storey steel buildings, and logistics hubs due to their rapid construction speed, cost savings, architectural flexibility, and spans of up to 90 meters column-free.

The cost of a PEB building in India typically ranges from ₹250 to ₹650 per sq. ft. for standard structural steel supply and installation, and ₹800 to ₹1,800+ per sq. ft. for comprehensive turnkey industrial projects (including foundation engineering, high-load flooring, insulation, and architectural facades).

Key factors influencing final pricing include:

  • Clear-Span Width & Height: Wider column-free spans (e.g. 40m–90m) and clear heights above 12m require heavier built-up section profiles.
  • Crane Capacities: Integration of overhead EOT cranes (e.g. 10 MT to 100 MT) adds crane runway girders and reinforced column sizing.
  • Wind & Seismic Parameters: Coastal cyclonic wind speeds (up to 200+ km/h) or Zone IV/V seismic zones require specialized stiffening.
  • Roof & Wall Cladding: Single-skin bare Galvalume vs. high-R-value PIR/Rockwool insulated sandwich panels.

Winsteel provides transparent, itemized BOQ estimations tailored to your exact architectural and structural load parameters.

A standard PEB facility can typically be designed, manufactured, and erected in 6 to 16 weeks, which is 40% to 60% faster than conventional civil RCC construction.

This accelerated timeline is achieved because 3D TEKLA detailing and factory fabrication happen concurrently with on-site excavation and civil foundation work. Once the anchor bolts are cast and cured, prefabricated columns, rafters, and purlins are bolted together seamlessly with crane assistance without curing delays or wet-trades.

Yes, PEB construction is substantially more cost-effective than RCC, delivering overall project cost savings of 20% to 30%.

Savings stem from multiple structural and financial factors:

  • Lighter Foundations: High-strength steel weighs 50% less than heavy concrete frames, reducing foundation excavation, rebar, and piling costs.
  • Zero Material Scrap: Computer-optimized CNC plate nesting ensures less than 2% steel waste, compared to massive wastage in on-site concrete casting.
  • Reduced Labor: On-site installation requires a streamlined installation crew rather than hundreds of manual shuttering workers.
  • Faster Revenue Generation: Delivering a facility 6 months earlier allows clients to commence manufacturing and logistics operations sooner, maximizing business ROI.

A professionally designed and properly maintained Pre-Engineered Steel Building has a design lifespan of 50 to 75+ years.

Winsteel structures utilize high-yield IS 2062 Grade E350/E450 steel, automated submerged arc welding, multi-coat marine-grade epoxy coatings, and premium AZ150 Galvalume roof sheeting (backed by 25+ year perforation warranties). Periodic inspection of rainwater drainage gutters and sealant beads ensures multi-decade longevity even in corrosive coastal and chemical manufacturing environments.

The PEB construction methodology follows a proven, precision-controlled 5-stage lifecycle:

  • 1. Structural Design & 3D Detailing: 3D TEKLA modeling and STAAD.Pro finite-element analysis in strict compliance with IS 800:2007 and MBMA standards.
  • 2. Precision Factory Fabrication: High-definition CNC plate cutting, automated Submerged Arc Welding (SAW) of tapered H-beams, and CNC roll-forming of high-tensile Z/C purlins in our Sachin GIDC plant.
  • 3. Protective Surface Treatment: Robotic shot-blasting to SA 2.5 standard followed by controlled spray application of zinc-rich epoxy primers and polyurethane finishes.
  • 4. Sequenced Jobsite Logistics: Numbered, barcode-tracked components delivered in exact installation sequence.
  • 5. Bolt-Together Site installation: Tandem hydraulic crane lifting, torqued High-Strength Friction Grip (HSFG) bolting, portal bracing, and 360° double-lock standing seam roof installation.

Winsteel utilizes certified, high-grade industrial materials for maximum structural integrity:

  • Primary Structural Members: High-yield steel plates conforming to IS 2062 Grade E350/E450 or ASTM A572 Grade 50 (yield strength 350–450 MPa).
  • Secondary Members (Purlins & Girts): Cold-formed high-tensile galvanized steel (minimum 275 GSM zinc coating, 345 MPa yield strength) for roof and wall framing.
  • Roof & Wall Panels: 0.50mm to 0.65mm high-tensile Galvalume (55% Aluminum-Zinc alloy coated sheet) with 150 GSM coating and durable exterior PVDF/SMP paint finishes.
  • Fasteners: High-strength Grade 8.8 and 10.9 structural bolts, nuts, and corrosion-resistant self-drilling fasteners with weather-tight EPDM sealing washers.
  • Thermal & Acoustic Insulation: Non-combustible Glasswool, Rockwool, or rigid PIR/PUF composite insulated sandwich panels.

The primary differences between Pre-Engineered Steel Buildings and Reinforced Cement Concrete (RCC) are:

  • Construction Speed: PEBs are completed 50% faster than RCC due to off-site factory fabrication running in parallel with foundations.
  • Clear-Span Spacing: PEB effortlessly delivers 30m to 90m column-free clear spans, whereas RCC is severely limited beyond 12–15m without massive, cost-prohibitive concrete beams.
  • Structural Weight: Steel PEB frames are 40%–60% lighter than bulky concrete, substantially reducing footing sizes and sub-soil improvement costs.
  • Future Modifications: PEBs can be easily expanded, dismantled, unbolted, and relocated. RCC is permanent, rigid, and impossible to relocate.
  • Seismic Safety: Steel's natural ductility and superior strength-to-weight ratio provide vastly superior resilience against earthquakes compared to brittle concrete.

While all PEBs fall under the broad umbrella of prefabricated construction, there is a fundamental distinction in engineering scope:

  • Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEBs): Heavy-duty, custom-engineered structural steel framing systems designed for massive clear-span industrial complexes, logistics hubs, heavy equipment plants, and multi-storey commercial towers. Each member is dimensionally tailored to specific architectural loads.
  • Generic Prefabricated Buildings: Typically refers to modular, lightweight portable units like site office porta-cabins, containerized living quarters, or light-gauge steel (LGSF) modular housing pods built to fixed, repetitive catalog dimensions.
