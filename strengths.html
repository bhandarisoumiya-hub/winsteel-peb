Pillars of Engineering Strength

Built on metallurgical rigor, automated heavy fabrication, and relentless quality control.

Engineering Precision & Design Mastery

Our in-house design engineering cell employs advanced 3D FEA (Finite Element Analysis) simulation and kinematic modelling to optimize weight, stiffness, and fatigue life of heavy structures.

Rigorous Quality Assurance

Accredited under ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 3834-2, we maintain uncompromised metallurgical standards, 100% traceability on all raw plates, and stringent multi-method NDT inspection.

Advanced Heavy Fabrication Muscle

With over 200,000 sq. ft. of covered manufacturing bays, 120-tonne tandem crane capacity, and high-definition automated cutting, we can build monumental structures under one roof.

Veteran Engineering Team

Guided by seasoned technocrats with over 35 years in structural bridge engineering, our 250+ workforce brings deep domain expertise to every fabrication challenge.

Unrivaled Delivery Timelines

Our integrated operations—from raw material stocking to in-house machining and coating—eliminate sub-contractor delays, ensuring on-time delivery on mission-critical infrastructure deadlines.

Safety First Culture (Zero Harm)

Strict compliance with ISO 45001:2018 safety standards ensures an accident-free environment across our manufacturing plants, testing bays, and site commissioning locations.

Zero Defect Protocol

Comprehensive Testing & NDT Accreditation

Every structure fabricated at Winsteel undergoes non-destructive examination (NDE) conducted by ASNT Level-II and Level-III certified inspectors. Our in-house metallurgy laboratory verifies chemical composition and mechanical impact resistance down to -40°C.

  • ✓ Charpy V-Notch Impact & Tensile Proof Verification
  • ✓ Full 3D Laser Tracker Coordinate Metrology
